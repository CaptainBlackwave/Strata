## 1. Overview

**Purpose**: Give players a reason to come back every day, with a shared puzzle and a streak.

**Scope**: Today's board, its weekly difficulty rhythm, the streak and tide passes, the reward, Share. The shell it grants is in `shell-collection.md`; coin handling is in `economy.md`.

**Current Implementation**: `generateDaily(dateKey)` builds a solver-proven board seeded by the date; `recordDailySolve` and `streakStatus` manage the streak; the home screen shows a card and the win card shows the result with Share.

**Design Intent**:
- **Everyone gets the same board on the same day** (the premise of Share). **Which day it is follows UTC, so players worldwide get the same board at the same moment** — `INTENDED — confirmed 2026-11-08`. **Implemented 2026-11-08** (`utcDateKey`, M4).
- Monday is easy and the week builds to a hard Sunday (S2-1).
- Streak coins grow with the streak; every 7 days banks a tide pass that saves one missed day; missing a day without a pass resets (S2-2).

## 2. Detailed Design

### 2.1 Core Mechanics

**Today** is the UTC calendar date (`utcDateKey`), so the daily switches at 00:00 UTC everywhere. In the Americas that is an afternoon or evening switch, and the card shows the UTC date (UX note for `design/ux/home.md`). A daily started before 00:00 UTC and won after it counts for the date it was started (`startDaily` fixes the date). Scenes follow the local hour instead (`scenes.md`).

**Weekly rhythm** (`daily.week`, index 0 = Sunday … 6 = Saturday; the weekday is computed from the date key itself, not the device):

| Day | Name | Colours | Capacity | Buried | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | hard | 6 | 8 (tall jars) | 0 | none |
| Mon | easy | 5 | 4 | 0 | none |
| Tue | easy | 6 | 4 | 0 | none |
| Wed | medium | 7 | 4 | 1 | none |
| Thu | medium | 8 | 4 | 1 | none |
| Fri | tricky | 9 | 4 | 2 | none |
| Sat | tricky | 10 | 4 | 2 | one, rotating weekly through narrow, frozen, locked, funnel |

All have 2 spare jars. The board is seeded from the date key and a salt (Formulas), then goes through the same solver-proven generation as numbered levels (`generateFromSpec`, 60 attempts).

**Never fails to load** (F7, *decided 2026-11-08, built in sprint 6*): the daily gets the numbered levels' fallback chain (drop the mechanic, then add a spare) and its failures are caught, so any date the device clock can reach loads a board.

**Streak** (`recordDailySolve`), where `gap` is days since the last solve:
- `gap == 1`: streak + 1.
- `gap == 2` and a pass is held: streak + 1, one pass spent (`usedPass`).
- `gap < 0` (the device clock was moved back, D10, **built in sprint 5**): the solve is **out of order**. No reward, no shell, no streak change and no reset (`outOfOrder`); the win card explains it.
- Anything else (including the first solve): streak = 1.
- If the new streak is a multiple of 7 and fewer than 2 passes are held: one pass is earned.
- Best streak is tracked.
- Only the **first** solve of a date pays; later solves on the same date change nothing.

**What a daily pays**: only the streak coins and a shell (or the 50-coin bonus once all 40 shells are owned, `shell-collection.md`). No level reward, no Perfect bonus, no tall-jar bonus (even on the tall-jar Sunday) and no "Perfect" title (`economy.md` §2.1). Boosters work as in a level, at the same prices.

**Home card** (`streakStatus`): shows today's difficulty, "Solved today ✓", the live streak, held passes, and "A tide pass will save your streak" when `gap == 2` and a pass is held. The card and the Shells button appear only after the tutorial is done. **After the clock moves back** (GA-DP-5, owner default 2026-11-08, *built in sprint 6*): the card shows today's real state, so "Solved today ✓" appears only if this date was actually solved (today `gap ≤ 0` always shows it).

**Win card**: "Daily solved!", pours and streak, the reward, a note if a pass was used or earned, the new shell, a Share button, and a Home button.

**Share**: native share sheet, else the clipboard: `Strata Daily <date> (<difficulty>)`, `Solved in N pours · S-day streak`, then the game's address. Contains no board spoilers. **N is the pour count of the first counted solve of that date** (GA-DP-6, owner default 2026-11-08, *built in sprint 6*; the count is stored with the solve). A replay can't share a better count than the solve that counted.

### 2.2 State and Data

- `StreakState`: `last` (date key or null), `streak`, `best`, `passes`.
- `DailyConfig`: `seedSalt` 7351, `week[]`, `streak {baseCoins, coinsPerDay, maxCoins, passEvery, maxPasses}`.

**Persistence**: the streak state is saved (`save-data.md`). The daily board itself is regenerated from the date.

### 2.3 Integration Points

- **Depends on**: `level-generation.md` (`generateFromSpec`), `jar-mechanics.md` (Saturday), `economy.md` (coins), `shell-collection.md` (shell on first solve).
- **Depended on by**: `feel-audio-ui.md` (home card, win card), `save-data.md`, `player-profile.md` (streak, best streak and counted solves feed its stats and unlocks).

## 3. Formulas

| Formula | Expression | Variables | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streak reward | `min(100, 30 + 10·(streak − 1))` | streak ≥ 1 | Day 1: 30; day 4: 60; day 8: 100 (cap) |
| Weekday | `(dayNumber + 4) mod 7`, 0 = Sunday | `dayNumber` = whole days since 1970-01-01 | 2026-11-08 → Sunday |
| Saturday mechanic | `list[floor(dayNumber / 7) mod 4]` | | Advances one mechanic per week |
| Pass earned | `streak mod 7 == 0 and passes < 2` | | Streak 7 and 14 each bank one pass |
| Daily seed | `FNV-1a(dateKey)` computed with the offset basis `2166136261 XOR seedSalt` (the salt goes into the basis *before* hashing), then attempt mixing | `seedSalt` 7351 | Boards must stay identical, so the code (`daily.ts`) is the requirement |

## 4. Edge Cases

**Handled in Code**:
- ✅ Two solves on one date: the second pays nothing and changes no state.
- ✅ One missed day with a pass: streak continues, pass spent; two missed days or no pass: reset to 1 (held passes are kept).
- ✅ Passes are capped at 2.
- ✅ Weekday and rotation are computed from the date key, so they match on every device for the same key; every day for 8 weeks generates a solvable board.
- ✅ A bad date in a save is repaired to "no solve yet" (`save-data.md`).

- ✅ **UTC, not local date** (M4, fixed 2026-11-08): the home screen and `startDaily` use `utcDateKey`. Verified in the browser: a phone in UTC+14 whose local date was Monday 9 November got Sunday 8 November's board. Moving the phone's clock still changes the date, since there is no server time.

- ✅ **Clock moved backwards** (D10, **built in sprint 5**): a solve dated before the last solve is ignored for the streak (no reward, no reset); see 2.1 Streak.
- ✅ A daily started before 00:00 UTC and won after it counts for the date it was started.

**Decided 2026-11-08, built in sprint 6**:
- **A date's daily fails to generate** (F7): caught, with the level fallback chain. A sweep of at least 2 years of dates is retained as a test (today only 8 weeks are tested; Saturday is the riskiest day).
- **Home after the clock moves back** (GA-DP-5): shows today's real state.
- **Share after a replay** (GA-DP-6): shares the first counted solve's pours.

**Not Yet Handled**:
- None.

**Unclear**:
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-08 (D2): the Saturday mechanic stays the same for everyone, even before the player has unlocked it (intro card shown); Saturday is not gated by progress.
- ✅ Decided 2026-11-08 (D18): the Share link is the GitHub Pages address.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

**What Exists** (covered in `tests/unit/core/daily.test.ts`):
- ✅ The same date always gives the same board; different weeks differ.
- ✅ Every day for 8 weeks is solvable with nothing sealed at the start.
- ✅ Monday is the easiest, Sunday is tall-jar, colours never fall from Monday to Saturday.
- ✅ First solve starts a streak of 1 and pays the base; consecutive days grow it to the cap.
- ✅ A pass is earned every 7 days (cap 2); one missed day is saved by a pass; otherwise reset to 1 with the best kept.
- ✅ Saturdays each have exactly one mechanic and all four appear within 8 weeks.

- ✅ The daily is keyed by the UTC date; the same instant in four time zones gives the same key (`utcDateKey` tests).
