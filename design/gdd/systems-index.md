## Overview

Strata is a phone-first sort puzzle: the player pours layers of sand between jars until every jar is empty or sealed. Around that loop sit a generator that guarantees winnable levels, four jar mechanics, boosters and an economy of coins earned only by playing, a daily puzzle with a streak, a shell collection and four time-of-day scenes. There are ten systems, all implemented.
