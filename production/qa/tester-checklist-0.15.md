# Tester checklist: Strata 0.15 on iPhone Safari and Firefox

About 10 minutes. Play at https://captainblackwave.github.io/Strata/ on **an iPhone in Safari** (also try the Home Screen app if you installed it), and separately on **Firefox** (desktop or Android). Write down the device, browser and version at the top of your reply.

For each step, reply **OK** or say what went wrong, and send a screenshot if something looks off.

## 1. Opening the game (1 min)
- [ ] The game opens. If you played before, a "What's new: Version 0.15.0, Come back tomorrow" card shows **once**. Tap Got it.
- [ ] Your level, coins and streak are the same as before the update.
- [ ] Reload the page. The What's new card does **not** show again.

## 2. Home (1 min)
- [ ] The daily card shows your streak and a line starting "Next streak reward:" or "Your next daily solve collects your streak rewards".
- [ ] A "Today's goals" card shows three goals with progress such as "0/25" and "+50 when all three are done".
- [ ] Nothing is cut off at the screen edges, including the Settings button top right.
- [ ] Try once with your phone's text size set larger (iPhone: Settings, Display & Brightness, Text Size). Nothing overlaps.

## 3. Daily puzzle and streak reward (3 min)
- [ ] Solve today's daily. The win card shows your streak and coins.
- [ ] If your streak reached 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 or 100, or passed one of them, the win card shows a "Day N streak reward" row, or a new frame or plate.
- [ ] If a goal was "Solve today's daily", a "Goal done … +20" row shows.

## 4. Goals and a level (3 min)
- [ ] Play one or two levels. The Home goals card counts up (jars sealed, levels cleared).
- [ ] When a goal finishes, its win card shows "Goal done … +N", and your coins go up by that amount.

## 5. Share (1 min)
- [ ] On a win card, tap Share. The share sheet opens (Safari) or the text is copied (Firefox desktop). The picture or text names Strata and your result.

## 6. Offline (1 min)
- [ ] With the game loaded, turn on Airplane mode (or go offline) and reload. The game still opens and a level can be played.
- [ ] Turn the network back on.

## 7. Anything else
- [ ] Anything slow, confusing or broken? Did any screen freeze for more than a second?

Reported issues go into Beads as bugs (`bd create ... -t bug`) with the device and browser in the title.
