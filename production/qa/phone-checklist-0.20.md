# Phone checklist for 0.20 (about 10 minutes)

Open https://captainblackwave.github.io/Strata/ on your phone. If an older version is cached, open it once, go back to Home, and it updates (the "Version" line in What's new should say 0.20.1 or later).

Reply with the number and ✅ or ❌, plus a word on anything that looks off.

## Install
1. In the browser (not installed yet), clear levels until you've cleared 5 in total. On Home a card "Add Strata to your home screen" appears. (Already past 5? It shows on your next visit to Home, at most twice, a week apart.)
2. Android: tap the card's Install button; the browser's own install prompt opens. iPhone (Safari): the card shows three step pictures: Share, Add to Home Screen, Add.
3. Install it. Open Strata from the home-screen icon: the icon looks sharp, a sandy splash shows briefly, and the game opens without the browser's address bar.
4. In the installed app, the install card never shows again.

## Offline and updates
5. Settings shows "Works offline".
6. Turn on airplane mode, close Strata fully, open it again from the icon, and play a level to the win. Turn airplane mode off.
7. (Only when a new version comes out) Home shows a bar "New version ready · Update". Tapping Update reloads into the new version on Home.

## Native feel
8. On a board, pinch with two fingers and double-tap quickly: the board doesn't zoom.
9. Long-press a jar or a button: no copy or select menu pops up.
10. The phone's status bar colour matches the top of the scene. Change the scene (Collection → Scenes) and it follows.
11. Android only: with Settings → Vibration on, a pour, a sealed jar and a win each give a light buzz. Turn Vibration off, and they don't. (iPhone browsers can't vibrate, so the switch is hidden there.)

## Look
12. Home: the three logo jars in Realistic sand, still, with a small sparkle now and then.
13. Settings → Realistic sand off: Home shows the plain logo jars, and boards are Classic.
14. Today tab: the weekly set card shows five small jars in a row, the week's theme as its title, and everything fits on screen.
15. Realistic board: after a pour, the sand in the target jar settles into a smooth slope, not little steps.
16. On a big level (about 14–16 jars), pours feel smooth, not stuttery. If they stutter, say which level.
