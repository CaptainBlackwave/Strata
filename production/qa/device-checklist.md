# Device checklist (owner, D58)

About 10 minutes on your own phone, on the live game (GitHub Pages). For each line, tick it or note what you saw. iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome) each help if you have both.

## Smoothness (heavy scenes)
- [ ] Buy or select **Aurora night** (Settings → Scenes). Play a level: the board stays smooth while the aurora moves, and the phone doesn't get warm within 2 minutes.
- [ ] The same with **Winter lights**.
- [ ] Win a level in **New Year fireworks**: the win-card bursts play smoothly and the card text stays readable.

## Zoom and tapping
- [ ] Pinch with two fingers on Home: the page zooms. Pinch back out.
- [ ] Tap jars quickly several times on the board: the page never zooms by itself (no double-tap zoom).
- [ ] At level 100 or later, every jar is easy to hit with your thumb, and no tap lands on the neighbouring jar.

## Text size
- [ ] Set your phone's text size to the largest, then open the game: Home, a board, the win card and Settings are readable, and nothing is cut off or hidden under the toolbar.

## Screen reader (optional, if you use one)
- [ ] VoiceOver (iPhone) or TalkBack (Android): pour sand; you should hear "Poured 1 … into jar N". Seal a jar; you should hear "Jar N sealed".
- [ ] Use a Hint: you should hear "Hint: pour jar N into jar M".

## Offline
- [ ] Open the game once online, turn on airplane mode, close the game and open it again: it loads and you can play the next level.

## Updates
- [ ] After the next update, the **What's new** card shows once, then never again.

Report anything that isn't ticked, with the phone model and browser. Screenshots help.
